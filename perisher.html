💡 Good to Know

🎿 Lift Passes
Book Perisher lift passes online in advance — you'll save money vs buying at the window. Multi-day passes offer the best value across your 3 ski days.
🚗 Getting to Perisher
From Dalgety it's ~45 mins via Jindabyne. The Skitube from Bullocks Flat (near Jindabyne) also runs directly to Blue Cow & Perisher Valley — great to avoid parking hassles.
👗 What to Pack
Thermals (top & bottom), waterproof ski jacket & pants, gloves, goggles, buff/beanie, and sunnies. Layers are everything! Boots come with hire if needed.
🌨 Snow Forecast
Mid-to-late August is Perisher's snowiest period. Check the Perisher daily snow report as you get closer and keep an eye on the forecast the week before.
🍔 Food & Snacks
Mountain cafes are pricey — pack snacks to save money. A packed lunch from the house is a great option, or budget ~$25–35 pp for an on-mountain lunch.
🧴 Sun & Altitude
UV is intense on snow even on cloudy days. Pack SPF 50+ and apply generously. The altitude will tire you out faster than usual, so stay well hydrated!
