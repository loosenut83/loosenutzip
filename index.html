i bought a domain name lol tuesday, 14 jan 2025 | 11:47pm

so yeah. i bought loosenut.zip. ten bucks. i have no idea what im going to do with it but here we are. it felt necessary.

been thinking about going back to basics lately. you know how websites used to just be... a page? someone's page? you'd stumble onto it through a webring or someone's links list and it was just some guy writing about whatever. no algorithm, no engagement metrics, no "content strategy". just a page.

anyway this is my page now. i'm going to write stuff here when i feel like it. no schedule. probably nobody will read it. that's fine actually that sounds great.

the .zip thing is a bit stupid and i love it.
