💡 Good to Know — Singapore

🚗 Getting Around
Download the Grab app before you leave Australia — it's Singapore's Uber equivalent and works brilliantly. Taxis are also readily available. Most rides across the city will cost SGD $10–30.
💰 Money & Currency
SGD $1 ≈ AUD $1.15. Singapore is cashless-friendly — credit cards are accepted almost everywhere. Hawker meals: SGD $4–10. Mid-range restaurant dinner: SGD $40–80 per person.
🌡️ October Weather
October is warm and humid — around 30°C with occasional afternoon showers. Pack light, breathable clothing, a small umbrella, and comfortable walking shoes. The humidity will surprise you!
🍜 Must-Try Dishes
Hainanese chicken rice, laksa, char kway teow, chilli crab, roti prata, bak kut teh, kaya toast with soft-boiled eggs, and mango sticky rice. Singapore is arguably the world's best food city — eat everything!
👗 What to Pack
Light, breathable clothing (linen is great), comfortable walking shoes, a light cardigan for air-conditioned restaurants and malls, SPF 50+ sunscreen, insect repellent, and modest cover-ups for temples. Pack one smart outfit for formal night onboard!
📱 Connectivity
Singapore has excellent mobile coverage. Consider an international roaming plan with your Aussie carrier, or buy a local SIM card at Changi Airport on arrival (~SGD $15 for unlimited data for 7 days).
🛂 Entry Requirements
Australians get visa-free entry to Singapore for up to 30 days — just your passport needed. Make sure all passports are valid for at least 6 months beyond your travel dates.
🎃 Halloween on the High Seas
Your October sailing includes Disney's Halloween theme — deck parties, Villains Night Out, characters in Halloween costumes, and themed menus. Pack something fun to wear for the deck party — even a simple costume accessory goes a long way!

🚢 Disney Adventure — Onboard Tips

⚡ First Hour Onboard — Do These Immediately
1.Connect to DCL Guest Wi-Fi — free, no internet package needed. This unlocks the Navigator app onboard. Do this before anything else.
2.🎢 Book Ironcycle Test Run time slot — top priority! Open the Navigator app immediately and grab a slot, OR run straight up to Marvel Landing on Deck 19 before the crowds settle in. This is the busiest attraction onboard and slots go extremely fast. Note: staterooms won't be ready yet — use this window to head straight up. Budget 4–6 minutes to walk from Deck 6 to Deck 19. If you miss embarkation day, try again during the 5:45PM dinner service window when most guests are at their tables.
3.Book Broadway show seats — open the Navigator app and lock in all four nights immediately. Popular time slots fill within the first hour.
4.Head to Senses Spa reception — secure your Rainforest Room passes before they sell out. Either visit the desk or book via the app. Unlimited 4-night pass is ~USD $179 per person — significantly better value than a single-day pass (~USD $79).
5.Book any remaining specialty dining — if Palo Brunch wasn't secured pre-cruise, head to the dining desk immediately.
6.Link all three of your phones to the stateroom in the Navigator app to unlock the free onboard chat — so you can message each other anywhere on the ship without using mobile data.
🍽️ Dining Tips
You can request dishes from previous restaurants — your serving team follows you each night and remembers your preferences. Order as many courses as you like, it's all included. Room service breakfast via door-hanger card is still free (leave it outside your cabin the night before) — perfect for a slow verandah morning. Note: other room service now has a USD $5 fee + 18% gratuity.
🌅 Make the Most of Your Verandah
Deck 9 Deluxe Oceanview with Verandah is a wonderful cabin. Watch Singapore's skyline disappear as you sail on embarkation evening. Morning coffee on the verandah on sea days is one of cruising's great simple pleasures. It's also a lovely quiet retreat when the ship is busy.
🎭 Don't Miss These
Lion King: Celebration in the Sky — the world's only fireworks show at sea, narrated by Shah Rukh Khan. Check the Daily Navigator for the night it runs and get a good deck spot early. Remember (the Wall-E musical) — reviewers say the whole theatre cried. Prioritise this one.
❤️ Use the Heart Feature
In the Navigator app, tap the heart on any activity you're interested in — it sets a 15-minute reminder before it starts. Great for deck parties and character appearances. Check the Daily Navigator each evening for the next day's schedule and plan your morning over dinner.
💆 Rainforest Room — Senses Spa
Adults-only thermal suite with steam room, sauna, aromatherapy showers, heated stone loungers with ocean views, outdoor hot tub, and an ice lounge. The Disney Adventure has no adults-only pool deck — this is the quiet adult retreat onboard. Ideal on sea days. Unlimited pass ~USD $179 per person for the full cruise.
👗 Formal Night
There'll be one formal night during your 4-night sailing — the rotational dining restaurants are beautiful settings for a dressed-up dinner. Pack one smart outfit each. Disney characters also appear in formal attire that night — great photo opportunity!
💰 Gratuities & Onboard Account
USD $14.50 per person per night is automatically added to your onboard account — that's ~USD $174 total for the three of you across 4 nights. Check your onboard credit balance in the app; it can be used for spa, specialty dining, drinks, and merchandise.
🎢 Cabin & Luggage Tips
Cabins may not be ready until 1:30–2PM on embarkation day — pack a small day bag with swimwear and what you need for the afternoon. Your main luggage will be delivered to your stateroom later. Do a thorough sweep of your cabin before disembarking — easy to leave things behind!
🚢 Future Cruise Tip
If you love the Disney Adventure and think you'd sail again, you can book a placeholder cruise onboard for just USD $200, which gives you 10% off your next Disney Cruise Line booking. You have 18 months to use it — worth doing before you disembark!
